> From a personal agent to an enterprise digital employee: AI takes a goal, uses tools, and returns work people can review.

Imagine leaving an assistant one message on Sunday night: “I’m meeting a customer next week. Pull together our previous conversations, the existing proposal, and the questions we still need to answer.”

What you want on Monday morning is a usable briefing: the customer’s background, relevant files, open questions, and a clear next step. The value is in moving the work forward while bringing decisions and approvals back to the right person.

## Muse puts cloud agents in the spotlight

In September, Meta introduced Muse as a personal AI agent. Meta says Muse runs in its own cloud environment, can develop a plan around a person’s goals, work across a browser and connected services, and continue longer tasks after the app is closed. Sensitive actions, including sending an email or making a purchase, require confirmation.

That changes the unit of work. A conversation can become a project with context, tools, and a series of actions instead of ending after a single answer.

Enterprise work adds another layer. Customer details may live in a CRM, conversations in messaging tools, and deliverables in documents or local files. A useful agent must work within those systems and their approval rules.

## Tego OS gives the agent a job

Tego OS is built around digital employees. Organizations can configure an agent for a role, run it in the cloud, give it skills and tools, and keep its tasks and deliverables in a project workspace.

![Four digital employee workspaces for research, sales, publishing, and CAD review](/images/cloud-agent/digital-employees.png)

A new Tego OS deployment can start with four roles: **My Assistant** for research, meetings, plans, and documents; a **Sales Process Assistant** for leads, opportunities, and pipeline work; a **Publishing Assistant** that can prepare a WeChat article through research, drafting, illustrations, formatting, quality checks, and draft submission; and a **CAD Review Engineer** that reads DXF drawings and organizes measurements, checks, evidence, and reports.

These roles are defined by what they deliver. A publishing assistant can turn a topic into a draft for an editor to review. A sales assistant can help the team see what an opportunity needs next. Drafts, documents, reports, and open decisions are more useful than a polished but disconnected answer.

## Work can continue through interruptions

Real work rarely finishes in one sitting. Information arrives later, requirements change, and people step away.

Tego OS keeps related conversations and files in a project workspace. Complex tasks can start with a plan and proceed after confirmation. Progress, questions, and results remain available when a user returns. Scheduled tasks and recovery of longer work support recurring processes.

Digital employees can collaborate through desktop, mobile, and messaging channels. With permission, they can use browsers, desktop software, files, and external services to act on a task.

## Enterprise agents need clear boundaries

The more an agent can do, the more clearly an organization must define its access. Who may use it? Which information may it see? Which actions need approval? Can the work be reviewed afterward?

![A person approving a sensitive action in a cloud-agent workflow](/images/cloud-agent/human-approval.png)

Tego OS supports user and team permissions, approvals, runtime management, and audit. Each user’s profile, long-term memory, and work journal are kept in an isolated workspace so a shared digital employee does not mix personal context across users.

Proven workflows can also become reusable skills. The skill workshop lets a team propose, review, and publish a workflow so knowledge gained in one job can benefit other digital employees.

## Start with one real task

Muse makes the potential of cloud agents visible to a broad audience. Tego OS brings that direction into enterprise roles, tools, workspaces, and permissions.

Start with a weekly task whose result is easy to check: prepare a customer briefing, create a sales follow-up list, or draft an article. Give a digital employee the goal, review what it returns, and decide what to delegate next.

**An agent becomes part of work when it can take a task, use the right tools, and return a result.**
