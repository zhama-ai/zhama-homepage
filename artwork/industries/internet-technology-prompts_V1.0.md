Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-width internet software solution website Hero background, 16:9.
Primary request: a premium technology visual for an internet product and growth solution. The subject is a precise digital network that carries user signals into coordinated product delivery. No lifestyle or park scene.
Scene: a cinematic spatial data-routing landscape, fine metallic optical pathways and clean translucent transmission filaments, three structured branches travelling through a deep blue-black space. Upper middle and center-right remain calm navy space to support a large white website headline and a separate product illustration overlaid in HTML. No bright subject in the center. Background should convey internet technology immediately without competing with the product content.
Style: premium cinematic 3D technology editorial art, precise optical materials, realistic gentle light, architectural depth, restrained energy, contemporary SaaS launch visual. Avoid stock-photo offices or hardware product advertising.
Palette: graphite midnight blue, cool steel, restrained cyan, a little indigo violet. No green, yellow, orange, warm beige or rainbow.
Constraints: absolutely no people, no trees, no lawns, no park, no city buildings, no fake screens, no dashboards, no empty UI rectangles, no skeleton bars, no pseudo-text, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. No spheres, globes, rings, chrome blobs, robots or holographic brains. No excessive neon bloom, no starfield, no sci-fi spaceship. Absolutely no independent decorative top horizontal line or top accent strip, no page numbers or page counters.
